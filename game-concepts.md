## Game Concepts

### 1. Chrono-Scroll
**Core Gameplay Loop**: Collect time fragments while navigating through shifting timelines where past events influence the present

**Unique Mechanic/Template**: Non-linear timeline mechanics where you can manipulate events in the past to solve present challenges

**Brief Description**: In a modular city where history repeats like broken records, you play as a "Chrono-Cutter" who can rewind time snippets to extract crucial items, but each change creates ripple effects that alter the city's layout and available resources

**Documentation**: [chrono-scroll.md](./chrono-scroll.md)

### 2. Echo-Babel
**Core Gameplay Loop**: Solve spatial puzzles using multilingual sound cues and frequency modulation

**Unique Mechanic**: Sound-based puzzle solving where different languages produce different visual distortions and physics effects

**Brief Description**: Explore an ancient ruin where language is the key to reality manipulation. The world responds to the frequency patterns of spoken words, creating shifting platforms, opening portals, and revealing hidden mechanisms when you speak ancient dialects

**Documentation**: [echo-babel.md](./echo-babel.md)

### 3. Neural Dawn
**Core Gameplay Loop**: Build and upgrade neural networks to manipulate autonomous agents in a sci-fi city

**Unique Mechanic**: Brain-computer interface simulation where player thoughts can directly command drone swarms and digital entities

**Brief Description**: Play as a mind-architect in a cybernetic metropolis where you design neural pathways to control various agents. Each creation responds emotionally and personally to your design choices, leading to complex consequences across the city

**Documentation**: [neural-dawn.md](./neural-dawn.md)

### 4. Ecology Paradox
**Core Gameplay Loop**: Modify ecosystem parameters to achieve specific outcomes in a post-apocalyptic landscape

**Unique Mechanic**: Dynamic ecosystem simulator where predator-prey ratios, climate zones, and biodiversity drive emergent gameplay

**Brief Description**: In a world where human intervention is forbidden, you must carefully balance ecosystem restoration in a former industrial wasteland. Every decision shapes biomes, wildlife behavior, and human settlements that may or may not return

**Documentation**: [ecology-paradox.md](./ecology-paradox.md)

### 5. Mimetic Market
**Core Gameplay Loop**: Trade and craft using skills borrowed from people you encounter

**Unique Mechanic**: Memory absorption where you can "copy" abilities by observing others' actions

**Brief Description**: Navigate a maze-like bazaar where every stall trader embodies a specific craft (carpentry, performance art, engineering). By watching their techniques and mentally replicating their movements, you unlock new recipes, building techniques, and social strategies for advancing in this community

**Documentation**: [mimetic-market.md](./mimetic-market.md)
